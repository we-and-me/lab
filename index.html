Why the Lab works

A committed community beats a floundering DIY community every time.

Think about the last event where the energy was really high. Someone started a WhatsApp thread or a group text so it wouldn’t end. A few weeks later, it went quiet.

The Lab is built so that doesn’t happen. There’s a session on the calendar every month, and everyone in the room has chosen to keep practicing and try things they haven’t tried before.

Questions

When does the Lab meet?

The last Wednesday of each month for two hours, starting at 11am ET (8am PT, 5pm Central Europe). The first session is January 27, 2027: The AI Swap.

What if I can’t make it live?

Every session is recorded and stays in the Lab for as long as you’re a member. The Practice Floor works best live, so come when you can.

Can I join later?

No. The doors open for one week only, after the Summit or the Masterclass. This door closes Friday, December 18.

Is the Summit seat really included?

Yes. Every year you’re a member, your Connectors Summit seat is included.

I was a Lab member last year. Is this included?

No. You were part of the very first Lab experiment after the 2025 Connectors Summit. To stay a member, you need to register now, or you’ll lose access to everything.
